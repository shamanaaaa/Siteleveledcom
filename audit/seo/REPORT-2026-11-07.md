## 1. Executive summary

**The technical foundations are now in good shape.** The crawl and indexing checks that failed on 2026-11-01 now pass on the live site: real 404s in both languages, one redirect hop to the canonical URL, self-referencing canonicals, a clean sitemap and robots.txt, pre-rendered HTML with text and links, and one descriptive h1 per page. The English version is set up correctly: hreflang is reciprocal on all 20 pages with no errors, `lang` and `og:locale` match each page, noindex pages carry no hreflang, the language is never switched automatically, and no Slovak text was left on the English pages. Lighthouse mobile gives SEO 100 and accessibility 100 on every page tested, with performance 89–99.

What holds the site back now is no longer anything crawlers trip over. It's what Google has to work with once it has indexed the pages:

1. **No measurement** (G-01). There's no analytics or conversion tracking, and this audit had no Search Console access, so it's unknown which of the 20 URLs are indexed or what they rank for.
2. **No outside authority** (G-02). None of the 8 portfolio sites links to siteleveled.com, including the two that are DOMAN s.r.o.'s own sites. The Facebook link goes to a personal profile, not the business page that holds the reviews.
3. **No local signal** (G-03). The company is registered near Nitra and the portfolio shows projects in Nitra and Pezinok, but no page names a service area, and there's no known Google Business Profile.
4. **Only commercial pages** (G-04). The blog is a noindex placeholder and there are no case studies, so the site can't rank for any informational query.
5. **English titles aim at global head terms** (G-05) such as "Custom websites and online stores". A new 10-page English section can't rank for those without a qualifier.

**Quick wins** (under an hour each, no new pages needed): credit links on mdfoto.sk and fotkynavyber.sk (G-02), fixing the "every card is a real client" line (G-06), address and profile links in the structured data (G-07), and the English title and description edits (G-05, G-10).

## 3. Findings

### G-01 · No analytics, and indexing hasn't been checked · **High** · owner + code

- **Issue.** There is still no analytics or conversion tracking in `src/`, `index.html` or `server/` (F-07 from the first audit). The sitemap was submitted on 2026-11-01 and the English pages went live on 2026-11-04, but no one has looked at what Google actually indexed.
- **Impact.** It can't be shown whether any change since 2026-11-01 brought visitors or leads, or whether a lead came from search, an ad or outreach. Indexing problems, such as Google folding an English page into its Slovak twin, stay invisible.
- **Observed later on 2026-11-07** (owner's screenshot of Search Console → Indexovanie → Strany, Domain property `siteleveled.com`): 6 known pages, **3 indexed** and **3 not indexed**: "Stránka s presmerovaním" (page with redirect) ×2 and "Duplikovať bez kánonickej adresy vybranej používateľom" (duplicate without user-selected canonical) ×1, validation not started. The chart's daily bars end around **23–24 Oct 2026** (read from the axis, not hovered), so the report shows the site **before** the 1 Nov fixes and the 4 Nov English release. That fits the old link structure, where only `/`, `/kontakt` and `/seo-audit` could be found (F-01). Redirecting URLs are expected to stay unindexed, and on today's site every indexable page has a canonical, so neither reason points to a current defect. The 20-URL sitemap isn't reflected in that report yet. The Sitemaps report (owner, same day) shows `https://siteleveled.com/sitemap.xml` submitted 1 Nov, **last read 5 Nov, status "Úspech", 20 pages discovered**. Google has therefore read the version with the English pages (the file's Last-Modified is 4 Nov 14:03), and no resubmission is needed. Discovery is done; indexing should show up in the Pages report once that report catches up.
- **Fix.**
  1. *Owner, about 15 minutes:* in Search Console, open **Pages** and look for "Discovered/Crawled – currently not indexed" and "Duplicate, Google chose different canonical" on any of the 20 URLs. Run **URL Inspection** on `/en/`, `/tvorba-eshopu/` and `/en/online-stores/`, and request indexing for any that are missing. In **Performance**, note the queries and positions as a baseline.
  2. *Code:* add analytics that keeps the privacy policy true. The policy promises **no cookies** and says the site contacts **no third party**, so either self-host a cookieless tool on the VPS (Umami or Plausible Community Edition) or update the policy first. Send one event when `/api/navrh` answers 200, labelled with the form it came from (home, `/kontakt/` or the e-shop page) and the language.
- **Verify.** A test enquiry shows up as one conversion with the right source and language.

### G-02 · No links from the portfolio sites, and the wrong Facebook link · **High** · owner

- **Issue.** None of the 8 portfolio sites links to siteleveled.com (`client-backlinks.txt`, same result as on 2026-11-01). Two of them, mdfoto.sk and fotkynavyber.sk, are DOMAN s.r.o.'s own sites, and sanifix.sk is built and maintained in a repository the owner controls. The site's Facebook icon still points to the personal profile `facebook.com/mario.doman`, not the business page that holds the three reviews (F-23).
- **Impact.** A 20-page domain with no links has very little authority. Links from real client sites are relevant, easy to get, and also act as proof that the work is real.
- **Fix.**
  1. Add a small footer credit, "Web: SiteLeveled", linking to `https://siteleveled.com/` on mdfoto.sk and fotkynavyber.sk now, then on sanifix.sk with the client's OK. Use the brand name as the link text, not a keyword such as "tvorba webstránok": keyword-rich footer links across many sites are what Google's link-scheme policy targets.
  2. Ask the other five clients for the same credit, starting with the three who already recommended SiteLeveled on Facebook.
  3. Replace the Facebook link with the business page URL in the footer and on `/kontakt/`, and add the same URL to `sameAs` (G-07).

### G-03 · No service area or Google Business Profile · **Medium–High** · owner + content

- **Issue.** The registered office is in Cabaj-Čápor (Nitra district). The portfolio shows projects in Nitra (springnitra.sk, zeriavynitra.sk) and Pezinok (jogaslaskou.sk), and the services page offers clients "Lokálne SEO (Google Business, mapy)". Yet the Slovak pages never name an area. "Nitra" appears only inside client descriptions, and the only area statement is "Slovensko (pracujeme remote, celá SR)" on `/kontakt/`. No Google Business Profile is linked or known.
- **Impact.** Searches like "tvorba webstránok Nitra" usually show a map pack first, and only businesses with a profile can appear in it. Without one, and with no area named on the site, the strongest local opportunity is left unused. Map ranking depends on how far the searcher is from the business, so no position can be promised.
- **Fix.**
  1. *Owner:* create or claim a Google Business Profile as a **service-area business**. Hide the address if customers don't visit it, set the service area (Nitra region and Slovakia), choose a primary category such as "Website designer", and use the website URL `https://siteleveled.com/`. Ask the three Facebook reviewers whether they would also review there, using a neutral request with no incentive.
  2. *Content:* say it once, truthfully, where people look for it: on `/kontakt/` and in the home "O nás" block. Example, built only from facts already on the site: "Sídlo máme v Cabaji-Čápore pri Nitre a pracujeme pre firmy z celého Slovenska." If the owner also meets clients in person in Nitra, add that, but only if it's true.
  3. *Schema:* G-07.
  4. Don't build city pages that only swap the town name. One genuine Nitra page makes sense only once there's local content for it, such as Nitra case studies.

### G-04 · No informational content and no case studies · **Medium** · content (owner)

- **Issue.** All 20 indexable pages are service pages. `/blog/` is still `noindex` with five placeholder posts that have no articles behind them. There's no English blog. The case studies proposed in F-21 don't exist yet.
- **Impact.** Questions people search before they buy ("koľko stojí webstránka", "e-shop alebo web", "prerábka starého webu") only have `/cennik/` to compete with. Case studies are also what turns the three Facebook reviews into proof a visitor can check.
- **Fix.**
  1. Remove the placeholder posts. The page isn't indexed, but a visitor who opens it sees articles that don't exist.
  2. Publish the first two or three articles from REPORT.md §6.3, each at its own `/blog/<slug>/` route with an entry in `src/app/seo/pages.ts`. Then remove `noindex` from `/blog/` and link it in the footer. Good first picks are "Koľko stojí webstránka na Slovensku" (linking to `/cennik/`) and the jogaslaskou.sk / springnitra.sk redesign story (linking to `/sluzby/`), because they come from real projects.
  3. With consent, turn the three reviewed projects into `/portfolio/<client>/` case studies: the brief, what was built, before and after, the review quote, and a link to the live site.
  4. Translate an article into English only if it suits international clients. Hreflang should pair an article only when both language versions exist.

### G-05 · English titles aim at global head terms · **Medium** · content + code

- **Issue.** The English titles use broad phrases such as "Custom websites and online stores", "Website development, SEO, and maintenance" and "Custom online store development". None of them says where the studio is or what makes it different. The plan's audience is international clients, without implying past international work (`docs/english-version-plan.md`).
- **Impact.** A new 10-page English section has almost no chance on global head terms. Searches it could realistically win carry a qualifier the business can truthfully claim, such as Slovakia or the price.
- **Fix** (hypotheses: no keyword tool was available, so check against Search Console queries 4–8 weeks after the change):

| Page | Current title | Proposed title (characters) |
|---|---|---|
| `/en/` | Custom websites and online stores \| SiteLeveled | Web design and development from Slovakia \| SiteLeveled (54) |
| `/en/services/` | Website development, SEO, and maintenance \| SiteLeveled | Web design, online stores and SEO \| SiteLeveled (47) |
| `/en/pricing/` | Website and online store pricing \| SiteLeveled | Website and online store pricing from €300 \| SiteLeveled (56) |
| `/en/online-stores/` | Custom online store development \| SiteLeveled | Online store development from Slovakia \| SiteLeveled (52) |

"From Slovakia" says where the work is done without suggesting the studio serves Slovakia only or has worked abroad. The titles live in `EN_META` in `src/app/seo/pages.ts`. If the English home h1 changes too, re-check its line breaks at widths 320–480 px with fonts held back, because a wrap change caused a 0.22 layout shift before.

### G-06 · "Every card is a real client" on the Slovak home page · **Medium (trust)** · content

- **Issue.** The portfolio intro on `/` says "Každá karta je skutočný klient a skutočný web, ktorý si môžete hneď otvoriť." (`PortfolioSection.tsx:168`). mdfoto.sk and fotkynavyber.sk are DOMAN s.r.o.'s own sites: their own structured data names the company (F-24). The English line is already accurate: "Each card is a real project for a Slovak business."
- **Impact.** A visitor who checks mdfoto.sk's operator finds the claim untrue. Trust signals weigh more than usual for a studio this small.
- **Fix.** "Každá karta je skutočný projekt a skutočný web, ktorý si môžete hneď otvoriť." Optionally add a "vlastný projekt" label to those two cards. Update the matching `en.json` key, which is the Slovak sentence itself, so the English text stays linked.

### G-07 · The structured data leaves out what the footer shows · **Low** · code

- **Issue.** Every page's footer shows the legal name, IČO 47 912 383 and the address, but the Organization schema has none of them (a comment in `schema.ts` says to leave them out until confirmed). The IČO has since been checked in the registry (2026-11-04). There's no `sameAs`. The English Organization has no `contactPoint`, although English enquiries go to info@siteleveled.com.
- **Impact.** Small, but it helps Google connect siteleveled.com with DOMAN s.r.o. and with a future Business Profile. It also only repeats facts the page already shows.
- **Fix** (in `SITEWIDE` in `src/app/seo/schema.ts`, owner OK for the address):

```json
"address": { "@type": "PostalAddress", "streetAddress": "Ulica Funduše 1165/26", "addressLocality": "Cabaj-Čápor", "postalCode": "951 17", "addressCountry": "SK" },
"identifier": { "@type": "PropertyValue", "propertyID": "IČO", "value": "47912383" },
"areaServed": { "@type": "Country", "name": "Slovensko" },
"sameAs": ["<Facebook business page URL>", "<Google Business Profile URL, once it exists>"]
```

Add `"sameAs": ["https://www.linkedin.com/in/m%C3%A1rio-doman-65b08663/"]` to Mário's Person node, since that profile is already linked on the site. On English pages, keep an email-only `contactPoint` with `"availableLanguage": ["en", "sk"]`. Add `vatID` only if the owner wants the VAT ID published, because the site doesn't show it today. Once the profile exists, `Organization` can become `ProfessionalService`.

### G-08 · English pages download the whole dictionary · **Low** · code

- **Issue.** Every English page loads `en-*.js`, 26 kB compressed, with the translations for all pages, and React waits for it before it attaches. Compressed JS and CSS: `/en/` 215 kB against `/` 189 kB.
- **Impact.** Small. Lighthouse `/en/` performance was 89–91 against 90–92 on `/`, within run-to-run noise. Blocking time on the home page (160–360 ms here) is still the larger, already-recorded speed problem (REPORT.md status block).
- **Fix.** Split `en.json` by page (shared strings plus one file per page) and load only the current page's file, using the same `pageCode` map `seoPages.ts` already uses for preloads. Measure before and after with real throttling (`--throttling-method=devtools`).

### G-09 · Images without width and height · **Low** · code

- **Issue.** 8 images on each portfolio page and 1 on home, `/o-nas/` and `/tvorba-eshopu/` (and their English twins) have no `width`/`height` (the rest of F-27).
- **Impact.** Measured CLS is 0–0.016, so nothing visible today. The attributes prevent future layout shifts if styles change.
- **Fix.** Add the attributes (the image files have fixed sizes) or `aspect-ratio` in CSS.

### G-10 · Short English descriptions · **Low** · content

| Page | Now | Proposed (characters) |
|---|---|---|
| `/en/about/` | 94 characters | Meet the people behind SiteLeveled: developer Mário Doman and DOMAN s.r.o., a Slovak company. Work directly with the developer who builds your website. (151) |
| `/en/services/` | 109 characters | Custom websites, online stores, copy, SEO and maintenance, built in Slovakia. Websites from €300, online stores from €1,000. Request a free proposal. (149) |

Google often rewrites descriptions anyway. These give it better text to start from.

### Optional polish (no finding)

- `sitemap.xml` has no `<lastmod>`. Add it only if it comes from real content dates (for example the git date of each page's source). Google ignores lastmod values that aren't reliably accurate, and with 20 URLs it adds little.
- Czech browsers get the Slovak suggestion banner, but Czech searchers fall under `x-default`, which points to English. That's correct hreflang: don't label Slovak pages as `cs`.

## 6. Method and limits

**Tools:** curl over HTTP/2 at 1 request/s; Puppeteer (headless Chrome 155) parsing the saved raw HTML with JavaScript off and all network requests blocked (`scripts/parse-raw.mjs`); Lighthouse 12.6.1, mobile preset with simulated throttling, 2 runs each on `/`, `/en/`, `/tvorba-eshopu/`, `/en/online-stores/`, `/cennik/` and `/en/pricing/` on production, `/api/*` blocked; raw-HTML checks of the 8 portfolio sites; code reading at `efb21bb`.

**Rules kept:** no form submitted, no `/api/*` URL requested, `server/.env` not opened, no tracked file changed. Only this report, `scripts/parse-raw.mjs` and `raw/followup-2026-11-07/` were added.

**Not measured, and what it would take:**
- **Indexing, queries, positions:** only one screenshot of the Pages report was seen, with data ending around 23–24 Oct (G-01). Needs the Sitemaps report, the list of indexed URLs and the Performance report.
- **Google Business Profile:** whether one exists, and map-pack visibility for "tvorba webstránok Nitra". Needs the owner and a search from Nitra on a phone, with the date recorded.
- **Backlinks beyond the portfolio sites:** needs Search Console → Links, or a backlink tool.
- **Field Core Web Vitals:** the site is probably too small for CrUX data. Search Console's Core Web Vitals report will say.
- **Search demand for the proposed English titles:** no keyword tool was available, so G-05 is a hypothesis to check against Search Console queries.

**Lab-data caveat:** Lighthouse numbers come from one WSL machine in simulated mode. Use them to compare pages, not as what visitors experience.
